How Wellington Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

A little routine care is the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a thermostat set near 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your water heater? The serial number on the label usually encodes the manufacture date, and that date tells you a lot. Most tank heaters reach 8 to 12 years, heat pump models 10 to 15, and regularly descaled tankless units about 20. If your heater is beyond that point and is also showing rusty water, rumbling, or moisture at the base, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install once the tank fails.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a short, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water starts eating the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a flooded utility room.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Wellington

Whatever type of water heater your Wellington home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tanks usually give trouble through pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric units burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment piles up around the lower element. Tankless units throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
